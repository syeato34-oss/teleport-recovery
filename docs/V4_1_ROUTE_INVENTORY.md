# Teleport V4.1 — definitive content and route inventory

Generated from the current typed source configuration. The V4 baseline is the 39-route snapshot captured before V4.1 edits. No new pages have been deployed.

## Definitions

- **Directory listing:** an enquiry-area name, often linking to its regional anchor. It is not a standalone city page.
- **City landing page:** authored pickup/access/FAQ content with its own canonical route.
- **Service page:** a distinct recovery requirement and booking/equipment information.
- **City-service page:** a selectively authored local/service combination, not every possible pairing.
- **Advice guide:** recovery-planning information, not a promise of repair or specialist capability.
- **Indexable canonical page:** a real rendered route included in the production sitemap; this does not mean Google has indexed it.
- **Redirect:** a permanent alternative URL leading to one canonical; not an additional substantive page.

New advice topics are source-backed planning information, not promised services.
