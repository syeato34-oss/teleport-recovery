## Rules for future changes

- Preserve confirmed claims at their current strength. Do not casually remove them solely because supporting records are not stored in this repository.
- Do not strengthen an average into a promise, a coverage claim into guaranteed dispatch everywhere, or an arrangement into an assertion that Teleport owns every vehicle or employs every operator.
- Do not add reviews, customer totals, arrival-rate percentages, prices, awards, addresses or insurance claims without owner-approved evidence.
- Link each prominent guarantee statement to the full guarantee. Keep the limitations and statutory-rights sentence consistent across marketing, FAQs and legal pages.
- Obtain updated owner confirmation and retain supporting records before changing a material claim. Update this register and its review date when that happens.
